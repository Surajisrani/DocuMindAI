Northwind Analytics Employee Handbook - Remote Work and Benefits Policy

Section 1: Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing the 90-day probation period. During probation, all employees must work from the office. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, and employees must be reachable during these hours.

Section 2: Home Office Stipend
Northwind Analytics provides a one-time home office stipend of $500 to every full-time employee who is approved for remote work. The stipend can be used for a desk, chair, monitor, or similar equipment. Receipts must be submitted to the finance team.

Section 3: Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year. Parental leave is 16 weeks of paid leave for the primary caregiver.

Section 4: Information Security
All remote work must use the company VPN. Laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within 24 hours.

Section 5: Expenses
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date. Reports submitted after 30 days will not be reimbursed unless approved by a department director.
